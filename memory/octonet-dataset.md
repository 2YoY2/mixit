OctoNet (HKU, NeurIPS 2025 D&B): 12 modalities, 41 subjects (19–70 y), 62 activities, 3 scenes (office/lab/living room), NTP-synced. Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/hku-aiot/OctoNet — paper PDF at ~/Desktop/MixITxIMU/894_OctoNet_A_Large_Scale_Mult.pdf.

Specs relevant to [[roombody-project-state]]:
- WiFi: AX6000 TX → 4 RPi/AX200 RX, ch36 @5.18 GHz, 40 MHz, 2 ant/RX (8 links), 75.62 Hz, frame 2×114, ~95 GB. RX4 deliberately NLOS (3 cm wood) — shadowing/ε testbed.
- IMU: Xsens Awinda, 17 trackers, 60 Hz, 13×17/frame. MoCap: OptiTrack 3DKP, 120 Hz, 50 markers. Heart rate: Polar H10.
